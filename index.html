My research focuses on the analysis of solar observational data, particularly the diagnosis of transient phenomena in solar active regions using spectroscopic and imaging observations in the UV, EUV, and X-ray regimes. I apply advanced data analysis techniques in IDL and Python for the reduction, analysis, and interpretation of multiwavelength solar observations.
